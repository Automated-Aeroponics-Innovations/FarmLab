Raising a small wall.

Pots, lights, misters, and a leaf that decides.

An animated simulation of an aeroponic grow wall. Plants in pots grow under lights that follow a day cycle; misters pulse along a manifold; a stress index rises when plants dry and falls after a pulse. Status values report lab time, light level, mist state, mean leaf stress, chamber humidity and temperature, and the controller version. A field-notes view shows fourteen photographs of the lab with captions.

How to read the wall

This page runs a small model of FarmLab's control loop in your browser. It is a sketch of the mechanics, not the lab's own software.

potsPlants grow by light and mist.
